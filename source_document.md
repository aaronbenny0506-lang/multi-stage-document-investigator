# Incident Investigation Report
## Riverside Distribution Center : Warehouse Forklift Incident
### Case File #RDC-2026-0947

---

## Executive Summary

On September 14, 2026, an incident occurred in Aisle 4 of the Riverside
Distribution Center involving a forklift operated by warehouse staff. The
incident resulted in a minor injury and damage to shelving unit 4-B. This
report compiles witness statements, maintenance records and personnel
information gathered during the investigation. The incident occurred at
approximately 2:15 PM and was witnessed by two employees on the floor at
the time.

---

## Section 1: Incident Overview

Location: Aisle 4, Riverside Distribution Center, Building C
Date: September 14, 2026
Time: Approximately 2:15 PM
Forklift Unit: FL-118

At approximately 2:15 PM on September 14, the forklift unit FL-118, being
operated by Dana Whitfield, struck the end-cap of shelving unit 4-B while
turning to exit the aisle. Several boxes of inventory fell and Dana
sustained a minor injury to the left hand. The operator's forklift
certification had lapsed the previous month and was pending renewal at
the time of the incident. Two employees witnessed the event and were
interviewed as part of this investigation. Facility management was
notified and the aisle was cordoned off pending review.

## Section 3: Witness Statement - Employee Dana Whitfield

"I was moving a pallet from Aisle 4 to the loading dock. I was turning
the forklift to exit the aisle when the rear end caught the shelving
corner. I didn't think I was going too fast. My hand got caught between
the wheel and the frame when I braced against the jolt, nothing serious,
just some bruising and a small cut. I've been driving forklifts here for
three years and this is the first incident I've had. I want to note that
I had completed my recertification training back in March, so I'm not
sure why there's any question about my certification status. The whole
thing happened fast, maybe around 2:15 like everyone's saying."

---

## Section 4: Witness Statement - Employee Marcus Ilunga

"I'd just clocked in for second shift, that's around 2:45, sometimes a
few minutes either side depending on the time clock line, and I was
walking toward the break room when I heard a crash. Actually, thinking
about it more, it happened right after shift change, so closer to 3:00
PM if I'm being precise. I saw Dana's forklift had clipped the shelving
in Aisle 4. Boxes were down everywhere. I want to say something about
that guard rail on the end of Aisle 4 too, that thing had been loose for
at least two weeks. A bunch of us mentioned it to facilities more than
once. It wobbled every time someone walked past it. I'm honestly not
surprised something happened over there eventually. As for how many
people saw it happen, I think it was just me and maybe one other person,
but I didn't get a clear look at who else was around, I was still pretty
far off when it happened.

Also, for what it's worth, the emergency alarm never went off. I remember
because someone had to physically run over and shout for the floor
supervisor. If the alarm system was supposed to trigger automatically, it
didn't."
